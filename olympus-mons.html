Olympus Mons

The largest volcano in the solar system, located on Mars

Caldera formed by collapsed lava chambers.
Summit of Olympus Mons, the highest volcano in the Solar System.

Key Facts

  • Height: 22 km (13.6 miles) - about 2.5 times the height of Mount Everest
  • Diameter: 600 km (372 miles) - roughly the size of France
  • Base circumference: approximately 2,000 km (1,240 miles)
  • Volume: about 100 times larger than Mauna Loa, Earth's largest volcano
  • Age: Estimated to be about 200 million years old
  • Last eruption: Estimated between 2-25 million years ago

Olympus Mons is a shield volcano formed by many layers of fluid lava flows. Its size is possible because Mars lacks tectonic plates, allowing the volcano to remain fixed over a hotspot and grow to enormous proportions.
