Mars Rovers

Ingenuity

The first aircraft to achieve powered, controlled flight on another planet, serving as a technology demonstration for future aerial exploration.

Curiosity

NASA's car-sized rover designed to explore the Gale Crater on Mars as part of NASA's Mars Science Laboratory mission.

Spirit and Opportunity

One of the twin Mars Exploration Rovers, Opportunity holds the record for the longest distance driven on another world.
